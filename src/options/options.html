Recording

Your microphone is recorded and mixed with the meeting audio, then transcribed on this computer. Without it, only the other participants are recorded and your own words are missing from the summary.

The recording — your microphone mixed with the meeting audio — is uploaded to OpenAI to be transcribed, so a recording of your voice leaves this computer. To keep it on this machine, switch transcription to the local engine below.

Takes effect the next time you start recording.

Transcription

This is not the LLM above. Summarizing and transcribing are two different jobs with two different engines and two separate keys. Claude (Anthropic), AWS Bedrock and Ollama have no speech-to-text API at all: a key for any of them can summarize a meeting but can never transcribe one. Only the engines in this list transcribe.

Applies to whichever engine is selected above. Nothing detects this for you: the engine transcribes as if this language were spoken, so a meeting held in a different one comes back as wrong words rather than as a translation. Defaults to English, and takes effect on your next meeting — it changes nothing else.

Local Whisper

The model is downloaded once on first use and cached by the browser; later meetings transcribe without re-downloading. Speaker names come from live captions, not from the audio.

OpenAI transcription

Faster and more accurate than local Whisper, at a price: the meeting's audio is uploaded to OpenAI. Do not choose this for a confidential meeting — local Whisper keeps the audio on this machine.

This key is stored separately from the OpenAI key in the Provider section above, and is used only for transcription. The same OpenAI key works for both, but you enter it in each place.

Use a model that returns per-segment timestamps (whisper-1). Speaker names come from live captions matched against those timings, so a model without them costs you the names.

ElevenLabs Scribe transcription

Tells voices apart: where live captions miss who was speaking, the summary reads Speaker 1, Speaker 2 instead of Unknown speaker. The price is that the meeting's audio is uploaded to ElevenLabs, and ElevenLabs may keep it under its own terms. Do not choose this for a confidential meeting — local Whisper keeps the audio on this machine.

Use a Scribe model (scribe_v2). Speaker labels hold within one upload of up to an hour; if a recording needs more than one, each label says which part it came from.

Amazon SageMaker transcription

Sends the meeting's audio to your own SageMaker endpoint, running Qwen3-ASR from SageMaker JumpStart. The audio is uploaded to your AWS account, and what is kept there depends on how the endpoint is set up: with SageMaker data capture on, for example, the endpoint stores every request in S3. Do not choose this for a confidential meeting unless you control that account — local Whisper keeps the audio on this machine.

Qwen3-ASR returns words without timings. So the audio is sent in short pieces cut at pauses, and each piece gets its speaker name from the live captions.

Paste the block your tool prints: export AWS_… lines from the AWS access portal or aws configure export-credentials --format env, a credentials-file profile, or the JSON from aws configure export-credentials. They need sagemaker:InvokeEndpoint on this endpoint and nothing more. Long-term keys (AKIA…) are refused. The credentials are kept in memory only, never saved to disk, and are gone when you close the browser. If they expire before a meeting ends, its audio is kept so that you can paste fresh ones and retry.

Qwen3-ASR does not list Hebrew, Norwegian or Ukrainian. For a meeting in one of these, no language is sent and the model guesses, so expect wrong words.

Sends one second of silence, never meeting audio, with the values above.

Summary

Off by default — a summary you forward would otherwise tell the recipient which engine and model ran on your machine.

Templates are fully editable — {{transcript}} is replaced with the meeting transcript. Defaults answer in the transcript's language; edit one line to force English.

Editing optional — the defaults work as they are.
